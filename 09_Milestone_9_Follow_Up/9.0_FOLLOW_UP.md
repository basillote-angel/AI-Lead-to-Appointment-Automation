# Milestone 9 — Follow-Up

## Objective

Track what happens after a lead is contacted, stop follow-up when the lead responds or books, and remind the team when a lead needs attention.

Follow-up is controlled by deterministic workflow rules. It does not negotiate with the customer or send unapproved AI messages.

## Workflow States

Use these states consistently in the lead record:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `NEW` | Lead was stored but not yet contacted |
| `CONTACTED` | An approved first response was sent |
| `WAITING` | Waiting for the lead to respond or take the next action |
| `RESPONDED` | Lead replied |
| `FOLLOW_UP` | A follow-up action is due |
| `QUALIFIED` | Lead has been scored and classified |
| `BOOKED` | Appointment was booked |
| `CLOSED` | No further action is required |

## Stop Conditions

Stop scheduled follow-up when any of these is true:

- The lead has responded.
- The lead has booked an appointment.
- The lead is marked `CLOSED`.
- A human marks the lead as not requiring further contact.
- The maximum follow-up count is reached.

Do not continue sending reminders after a stop condition.

## Workflow

```text
Approved Response Sent
   ↓
Update Lead: CONTACTED / WAITING
   ↓
Wait Until Follow-Up Time
   ↓
Get Current Lead Status
   ↓
IF — Should Follow Up?
   ├── yes → Create Follow-Up Task or Send Approved Reminder
   └── no  → Stop
```

Customer-facing follow-up messages must remain approved communication. For the first version, notify the team about a due follow-up instead of automatically sending to the customer.

## Step 1 — Update the lead after approval

After a human approves and sends the first response, update the existing lead row:

- `status`: `CONTACTED`
- `follow_up_required`: `true` when a response is expected
- `next_follow_up`: a planned future timestamp
- `follow_up_count`: `0`

Use the existing `lead_id`. Do not create a new lead record.

## Step 2 — Wait for the follow-up time

Add an n8n **Wait** node. Configure it to continue at the `next_follow_up` time, or use a scheduled workflow that searches for due leads.

For a beginner test, use a short delay or a manually selected test time. Do not wait days during the first test.

## Step 3 — Check the current status

Retrieve the lead from Supabase using `lead_id` before taking action. This prevents the workflow from using stale data.

If the status is `RESPONDED`, `BOOKED`, or `CLOSED`, stop the branch.

If `follow_up_count` has reached the configured limit, stop and notify the team for manual review.

## Step 4 — Decide whether follow-up is due

Use an **If** node with deterministic conditions:

```text
follow_up_required is true
AND next_follow_up is less than or equal to now
AND status is not RESPONDED
AND status is not BOOKED
AND status is not CLOSED
AND follow_up_count is less than 3
```

The exact n8n expression depends on the node output. Use the expression picker and inspect the data before configuring the condition.

## Step 5 — Handle a due follow-up

For the first implementation:

1. Send an internal Gmail notification to the team.
2. Include the lead name, email, current status, follow-up count, and next action.
3. Increment `follow_up_count` by one.
4. Set `status` to `FOLLOW_UP`.
5. Set a new `next_follow_up` time only if another follow-up is appropriate.

Do not send an unapproved customer-facing message in this milestone.

## Step 6 — Handle a response or booking

When a customer response or booking is detected:

- Set `status` to `RESPONDED` or `BOOKED`.
- Set `follow_up_required` to `false`.
- Clear or ignore `next_follow_up`.
- Do not send the scheduled follow-up.

## Test Checklist

### Test 1 — Follow-up is due

Use a lead with:

- `status`: `WAITING`
- `follow_up_required`: `true`
- `next_follow_up`: a time in the past
- `follow_up_count`: `0`

Expected:

- The due branch runs.
- The team receives an internal notification.
- Count becomes `1`.
- Status becomes `FOLLOW_UP`.

### Test 2 — Lead already responded

Use `status: RESPONDED`.

Expected:

- No follow-up notification is sent.
- `follow_up_required` is false.
- The branch stops.

### Test 3 — Lead booked

Use `status: BOOKED`.

Expected:

- No follow-up is sent.
- The appointment path remains the source of truth.
- The branch stops.

### Test 4 — Maximum follow-ups

Use `follow_up_count: 3` with a due `next_follow_up`.

Expected:

- No automatic follow-up is sent.
- The team receives a manual-review notification.
- The lead is not silently abandoned.

### Test 5 — Invalid lead

Submit an invalid lead through the intake form.

Expected:

- It is rejected by Milestone 2.
- It is not stored or scheduled for follow-up.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Follow-up states are documented and used consistently.
- [ ] Contacted leads receive a follow-up state and timestamp.
- [ ] Due follow-ups are detected deterministically.
- [ ] Responded, booked, and closed leads stop follow-up.
- [ ] Follow-up count is incremented safely.
- [ ] Maximum follow-up behavior is handled visibly.
- [ ] Invalid leads do not enter follow-up.
- [ ] The first version uses internal approval/notification rather than unapproved customer sending.
- [ ] Due, responded, booked, maximum-count, and invalid cases are tested.
