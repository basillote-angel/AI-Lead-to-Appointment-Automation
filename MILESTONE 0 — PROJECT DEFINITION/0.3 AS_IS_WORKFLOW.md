# Voryn — AS-IS Lead Process

## Project
AI Lead-to-Appointment Automation

## Milestone
0.3 — Map Current Process

## Definition

AS-IS describes how Voryn currently handles leads before automation is introduced.

## Current Tools

- Website inquiry form
- Email
- Google Sheets
- Google Calendar

## Current Process

### Step 1 — Lead Submission
A potential customer submits an inquiry through Voryn's website.

### Step 2 — Email Notification
Voryn's VA receives an email notification containing the lead information.

### Step 3 — Lead Review
The VA manually reads and reviews the inquiry.

### Step 4 — Data Entry
The VA manually copies lead information into Google Sheets.

### Step 5 — Lead Qualification
The VA evaluates the lead based on service, budget, timeline, requirements, and available information.

### Step 6 — Response
The VA manually prepares and sends a response.

### Step 7 — Customer Response
The customer may respond, request additional information, or fail to respond.

### Step 8 — Follow-Up
If necessary, the VA manually tracks and performs follow-up.

### Step 9 — Appointment Scheduling
The VA coordinates availability with the customer and checks Google Calendar.

### Step 10 — Appointment Confirmation
The VA creates the calendar event and sends confirmation.

## AS-IS Flow

Lead Submission
→ Email Notification
→ VA Review
→ Manual Data Entry
→ Manual Qualification
→ Response
→ Customer Response
→ Follow-Up if Needed
→ Appointment Coordination
→ Calendar Event
→ Confirmation

## Human Responsibilities

The VA currently performs:

- Lead review
- Data entry
- Qualification
- Response preparation
- Follow-up
- Appointment coordination
- Calendar updates
