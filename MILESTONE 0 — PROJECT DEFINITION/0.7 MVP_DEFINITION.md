# Voryn — MVP Definition

## Project
AI Lead-to-Appointment Automation

## Milestone
0.7 — Define MVP

## MVP Version

Voryn MVP v1.0

## Objective

Build the smallest complete version of the automation that demonstrates the core business value of processing a new lead and moving a qualified prospect toward an appointment.

## Core MVP Workflow

Lead Capture
→ Validation
→ Lead Storage
→ AI Analysis
→ Lead Classification
→ Notification
→ AI Response Draft
→ Human Approval
→ Appointment
→ Confirmation

## MVP Features

### 1. Lead Capture
Receive a new lead from the selected lead source.

### 2. Validation
Validate required lead information.

### 3. Lead Storage
Store valid lead information.

### 4. AI Analysis
Analyze the customer's natural-language inquiry and produce structured information.

### 5. Lead Classification
Classify the lead using defined criteria.

Initial classifications:

- HOT
- WARM
- NEEDS_INFORMATION

### 6. Notification
Notify Voryn when important lead events occur.

### 7. AI Response Draft
Generate a personalized response using approved business information.

### 8. Human Approval
Allow a human to review important AI-generated responses before sending.

### 9. Appointment Path
Allow qualified leads to proceed toward appointment booking.

### 10. Appointment Confirmation
Record and confirm successful appointments.

## MVP Success Criteria

SC-01 — A test lead can enter the system.

SC-02 — Invalid or incomplete lead information is detected.

SC-03 — Valid leads are stored correctly.

SC-04 — AI produces structured lead analysis.

SC-05 — A lead classification is generated.

SC-06 — A personalized response can be generated.

SC-07 — A human can review and approve the response.

SC-08 — A qualified lead can reach the appointment stage.

SC-09 — Appointment information is recorded and confirmed.

SC-10 — The complete journey can be traced from lead submission through appointment.

## Excluded From Initial MVP

The following are not required for MVP v1.0:

- Advanced CRM integrations
- Multi-channel lead capture
- Complex AI agents
- Autonomous negotiation
- Advanced analytics
- Sophisticated lead nurturing
- Large-scale infrastructure
- Advanced reporting

These may be introduced in later versions if they provide meaningful business value.

## MVP Principle

The MVP should demonstrate a complete end-to-end business workflow rather than attempting to implement every possible automation feature.
