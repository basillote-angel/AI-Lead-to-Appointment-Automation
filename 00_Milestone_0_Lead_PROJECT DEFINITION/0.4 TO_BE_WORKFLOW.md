# Voryn — TO-BE Lead Process

## Project
AI Lead-to-Appointment Automation

## Milestone
0.4 — Design Future Process

## Definition

TO-BE describes how Voryn's lead-management process should operate after automation is introduced.

## Future Process

1. Lead submits website inquiry.
2. n8n receives the lead.
3. Lead information is validated.
4. Valid lead information is stored.
5. AI analyzes the lead.
6. Business rules and AI analysis are used to determine lead classification.
7. Lead is routed according to its classification.
8. AI may generate an approved response draft.
9. Qualified leads are directed toward appointment booking.
10. Appointment information is stored and confirmed.
11. Follow-up workflows handle leads that have not completed the desired action.
12. Exceptions and complex situations are escalated to a human.

## Automation Responsibilities

The workflow should automate or assist with:

- Lead capture
- Validation
- Data storage
- Information extraction
- Lead analysis
- Lead scoring
- Lead routing
- Follow-up scheduling
- Appointment workflow
- Confirmation
- Notifications

## AI Responsibilities

AI may assist with:

- Natural-language understanding
- Information extraction
- Lead summarization
- Classification assistance
- Response drafting

AI should not independently invent business information or make unrestricted business decisions.

## Human Responsibilities

Human oversight remains responsible for:

- Complex customer requests
- Negotiation
- Custom pricing decisions
- Unusual situations
- Important customer communications
- Final sales decisions

## Human-in-the-Loop

The system should provide a human escalation path when:

- AI confidence is low
- Required information is ambiguous
- Customer requests unusual information
- Pricing or negotiation is required
- A business decision requires human judgment

## TO-BE Flow

Lead
→ Capture
→ Validate
→ Store
→ AI Analysis
→ Lead Scoring
→ Routing
→ Response / Follow-Up
→ Appointment
→ Confirmation
→ Human Escalation When Necessary

## Core Design Principle

The system should automate repetitive deterministic work, use AI where language understanding is useful, and keep humans responsible for decisions that require judgment.
