# Voryn — Project Success Criteria

## Project
AI Lead-to-Appointment Automation

## Milestone
0.10 — Project Success Criteria

## Purpose

Define how we will determine whether the Voryn automation successfully solves the intended business problem and whether the project achieves its learning and portfolio objectives.

---

## 1. Business Success Criteria

### BSC-01 — Lead Capture
Valid leads can enter the automation successfully.

### BSC-02 — Validation
Invalid or incomplete lead information is detected and handled.

### BSC-03 — Storage
Valid leads are stored correctly without being lost.

### BSC-04 — AI Analysis
The system produces structured analysis of valid lead information.

### BSC-05 — Lead Classification
Every valid lead receives an appropriate classification.

### BSC-06 — Workflow Routing
Leads are routed according to defined business rules.

### BSC-07 — Response Generation
The system can generate a personalized lead response.

### BSC-08 — Human Approval
A human can review and approve important AI-generated responses.

### BSC-09 — Appointment Path
Qualified leads can proceed toward appointment booking.

### BSC-10 — Appointment Recording
Appointment information is recorded correctly.

### BSC-11 — Traceability
The progress of a lead can be traced through the workflow.

### BSC-12 — Failure Handling
Important workflow failures are detected and handled rather than silently losing data.

## 5. Definition of Done

Project 1 will be considered complete when:

1. The business problem has been addressed.
2. The MVP has been implemented.
3. The core workflow has been tested.
4. Important failure scenarios have been handled.
5. The system has been documented.
6. Deployment considerations have been addressed.
7. The project can be demonstrated as a portfolio piece.
8. The student can explain the architecture, workflow, tools, and business value.
