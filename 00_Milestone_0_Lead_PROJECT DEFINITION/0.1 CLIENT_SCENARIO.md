# Client Scenario

## Project
AI Lead-to-Appointment Automation

## Milestone
0.1 — Define Client Scenario

## Client
Voryn Studio

## Business Type
Small Web Design & Digital Marketing Agency

## Services
- Website Design/Development
- Website Redesign
- E-commerce Website
- Basic Digital Marketing

## Lead Source
Website inquiry/contact form

## Lead Information
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Company
- Service Needed
- Budget
- Timeline
- Message

## Current Lead Process

1. Customer submits inquiry form.
2. VA receives the inquiry.
3. VA reads the message.
4. VA copies lead information.
5. VA records the lead in a spreadsheet/CRM.
6. VA evaluates the lead.
7. VA writes and sends a response.
8. VA follows up if the customer does not respond.
9. Qualified leads are scheduled for a consultation/discovery call.

## Current Problems

- Manual data entry
- Manual lead qualification
- Repetitive responses
- Follow-up can be forgotten
- Lead prioritization is inconsistent
- Appointment scheduling requires manual coordination

## Desired Outcome

Create an AI-assisted automation that can:

- Capture leads
- Validate lead information
- Store lead data
- Analyze the inquiry
- Qualify and score the lead
- Route leads
- Assist with responses
- Follow up
- Schedule appointments
- Send confirmations and reminders

## Initial Automation Principle

AI will assist with tasks such as analysis, classification, summarization, and response drafting.

Deterministic workflow logic will handle validation, routing, data storage, and other rule-based operations.

Human approval will initially be required for important customer communications.

## Example Lead

### High-Potential Lead

Name: Maria Santos
Company: Santos Coffee
Service: E-commerce Website
Budget: ₱100,000
Timeline: 30 days

Example Message:

"We need a professional website for our coffee shop.
We want customers to see our menu, location, and
contact us online."

### Lead Qualification Considerations

The system may evaluate:

- Requested service
- Budget
- Timeline
- Intent
- Requirements
- Information completeness
- Potential fit with Voryn's services

## Example Lead Categories

### HOT
Lead appears highly qualified and should receive priority attention.

### WARM
Lead appears potentially suitable but may require additional information or follow-up.

### COLD / LOW INFORMATION
Lead does not currently provide enough information to determine fit.

Important:
A lead with insufficient information should not automatically be treated as a bad lead.

## Initial Automation Scope

The MVP will focus on:

1. Lead capture
2. Lead validation
3. Lead storage
4. AI-assisted lead analysis
5. Lead scoring/classification
6. Notification
7. AI-assisted response
8. Appointment scheduling

Additional capabilities such as advanced follow-up, reminders, monitoring, and more sophisticated AI behavior will be added after the core workflow is working.

## AI Decision Principle

AI will assist with analysis, classification, extraction, summarization, and response drafting.

Business rules will handle deterministic decisions.

Human oversight will remain part of the initial system for important customer communications and decisions.
